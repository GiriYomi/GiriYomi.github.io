Job Traces

Synthetic Job Traces (JSSPP'24)

GitHub Paper

Synthetic job trace datasets generated using machine learning methods (TVAE and CTGAN). These traces are designed to help researchers study HPC scheduling and resource management without requiring access to proprietary production traces.

Available Datasets:

Blue Waters TVAE-15K Blue Waters CTGAN-15K Theta TVAE-15K Theta CTGAN-15K Helios TVAE-15K Helios CTGAN-15K Philly TVAE-15K Philly CTGAN-15K
