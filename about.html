About DIRLab

Data Intelligence Research Lab at the University of Delaware

Our Mission

We develop innovative solutions to enhance the performance and reliability of data-intensive infrastructure. Our research focuses on high-performance computing systems, storage optimization, and intelligent resource management.

DIRLab is directed by Dr. Dong Dai, Associate Professor in the Department of Computer & Information Sciences at the University of Delaware. Our team includes PhD students, master's students, and undergraduate researchers working on cutting-edge problems in systems research.
